## About the Cloud Service 
Skypea Cloud Services runs the Skypea HR Assistant, a multi-tenant software platform that other businesses use to manage their employees’ records. Customers upload and process their staff’s employment, payroll, and benefits information on the platform. Skypea holds this customer personal data on its customers’ behalf, which makes Skypea a service organization. 

Earlier, Skypea used the NIST Cybersecurity Framework to assess its posture and knows where its gaps are. Now the driver is commercial: two enterprise prospects have said they will not sign until Skypea can show independent proof that its controls protect their data, and they specifically want assurance that the controls have operated effectively over time, not just that they look good on paper today. Skypea has decided to pursue a SOC 2 report 

## What Skypea Commits to Customers 
* Uptime: Skypea’s customer contracts commit to 99.9 percent platform availability. 
* Confidentiality: Skypea commits to protect customer business information it is entrusted with from unauthorized disclosure. 
* Personal data. Because the platform holds employees’ payroll and benefits information, Skypea commits to handling that personal information responsibly.

## What the Platform Does With Data 
The HR Assistant primarily stores, organizes, and shares customer records. It is a system of record and workflow. It does not compute payroll figures, run financial calculations, or produce transaction outputs that customers rely on for accuracy; customers bring their own figures and use Skypea to hold and route them. 

## Current Control Posture (from earlier NIST assessment) 
* Customer data is encrypted at rest and in transit. This is a genuine strength. 
* Backups run automatically every day, but their integrity has never been verified and restores have never been tested. 
* Staff sign in through the corporate identity provider with single sign-on, and multifactor authentication is enabled, but it is not enforced on three long-standing administrator accounts that still use password-only sign-in.
* The platform and identity provider generate logs, but the logs are not aggregated or monitored on any schedule. 
* Skypea depends entirely on one hosting provider, CloudScale Infrastructure, for the platform, and has no prioritized supplier inventory or due-diligence process. 
* A CISO is accountable for cybersecurity risk and reports to the CEO.
  
## The Ask 
Leadership wants to understand, before scoping begins: what type of SOC 2 report the prospects are really asking for, which Trust Services Criteria categories belong in that report, given what Skypea does and commits to, and which categories can reasonably be left out of the first report. 

## Reference: The Five Trust Services Criteria 
* **Security (the Common Criteria):** protection of systems and data against unauthorized access, disclosure, and damage. Required in every SOC 2 report. 
*** Availability:** the system is available for operation and use as committed. 
* **Processing Integrity:** system processing is complete, valid, accurate, timely, and authorized. 
* **Confidentiality:** information designated as confidential is protected. 
* **Privacy: **personal information is collected, used, retained, disclosed, and disposed of properly. 

## Reference: Report Types 
* **Type I:** reports on whether controls are suitably designed and implemented at a single point in time. 
* **Type II: **reports on whether controls are suitably designed and operated effectively over a period of time (commonly three to twelve months).
