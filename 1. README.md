### The Criteria to Map Against
Map each control to the criteria series it primarily supports, then find any series with no control mapped to it.

### Reference: Mapping
A criterion is an outcome to achieve. A control is something the organization does to achieve it. One control can satisfy more than one criterion, and one criterion can be supported by more than one control. A criterion with no control mapped to it is a gap: nothing in the environment is achieving that outcome.

### Reference: Where Skypea’s Controls Fit
Encryption and multi-factor authentication both restrict and protect access, which is the domain of CC6 Logical and Physical Access.

Daily backups support recovery and continued availability, which is the domain of A1 Availability.

A change management process is the domain of CC8 Change Management.

CISO accountability and reporting lines are the domain of CC1 Control Environment.
 

## 4. Readiness Assessment
### The Gaps
These are the gaps carried forward from the control mapping, written up for the readiness assessment. Each is described with the risk it presents and the effort to fix it.

### Reference: Severity	Scale
Low:	Minor gap, little practical risk
Medium: Gap with moderate risk
High:	Serious gap affecting important systems or data
Critical:	Gap that could directly lead to compromise of sensitive or regulated data
 

### Reference: Prioritization
Prioritize gaps by risk first: the higher the risk to sensitive data, the sooner it must be fixed.

Among gaps of similar risk, address lower-effort fixes first to close exposure quickly.

A gap that is both high risk and low effort is the clearest first move.
 

# Alignment With Industry Standards
Skypea seeks to align its controls with SOC 2 Trust Services Criteria:

* **Security (the Common Criteria):** protection of systems and data against unauthorized access, disclosure, and damage. Required in every SOC 2 report.

* **Availability:** the system is available for operation and use as committed.

* **Processing Integrity:** system processing is complete, valid, accurate, timely, and authorized.

* **Confidentiality:** information designated as confidential is protected.

* **Privacy:** personal information is collected, used, retained, disclosed, and disposed of properly.

It also focuses on the Security categories CC1 to CC9


# Verdict
An organization is ready to begin its audit period when its in-scope controls are designed, implemented, and operating, with no open critical gaps.

There are still concerns:

* MFA gap: Multi-factor authentication is not enforced on 3 administrator accounts
* Backup restores have never been tested
* Logs are not aggregated or monitored
* No vendor risk management for the hosting provider
  
Skypea has to choose which gap to remediate first, and judge whether they are ready to begin its audit period.

A Type II period should not begin while critical gaps are still open, because the period would record the control as failing.
